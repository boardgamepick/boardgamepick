Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25.10.2026 The data controller is Agata Kobusińska (contact: kobusinska.agatap@gmail.com). Board Game Pick helps you pick a board game from selected BoardGameGeek collection. 1. Ads and Third-Party Services The app requires no account or sign-in. It displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve ads. The app communicates with the Gemini API to process user requests, but does not collect or store personal data such as location, contacts, or photos on external servers. 2. Data stored on your device BGG usernames you add, game lists, your edits to game details and downloaded cover images are stored only on your phone. You can delete them by removing a collection in the app, clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. 3. BoardGameGeek To download a collection, the app sends the BGG username you enter and game IDs to the public BoardGameGeek API. Cover images are loaded from BGG servers. BoardGameGeek handles this data under its own privacy policy. 4. AI assistant The AI assistant runs only when you write to it. It then sends: your question, recent messages from the conversation, the list of games in the selected collection (names and game parameters) and your device language. This goes to our relay server (hosted on Render) and on to Google Gemini, which generates the recommendations. Your BGG username is not sent. Our server does not store your questions; it forwards them and returns the answer. Google processes this data under the Gemini API terms. Please do not include personal information in your questions. 5. Legal basis (GDPR) We process data to provide the feature you request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and in our legitimate interest in keeping the service running (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). 6. Security All network traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). We do not sell data or share it for marketing. 7. Your rights You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. For privacy questions, write to kobusinska.agatap@gmail.com. 8. Children The app is not directed at children under 13. 9. Changes We will announce changes by updating this page and its effective date.
